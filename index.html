What is this?
The computer starts with random colored blobs and tries to recreate the image on the left. It adjusts each blob by moving it, resizing it, and changing its color thousands of times until the result looks as close as possible to the target.

This is a simplified demo inspired by 3D Gaussian Splatting (Kerbl et al., SIGGRAPH 2023) to give an intuition for how the method works. The real paper is much more complex.
